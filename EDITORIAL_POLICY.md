# Editorial and Ranking Policy

## Neutral Directory Approach
Top Consultancy Nepal is designed as a neutral, trustworthy directory for education consultancies in Nepal. We strive to provide accurate, unbiased information to help students make informed decisions.

## Ranking Methodology
Consultancies listed on our platform are ordered based on neutral, data-driven factors, primarily:
- **Verification Status**: Verified consultancies appear higher than unverified ones.
- **Completeness**: Profiles with comprehensive details (services, locations, contact info) are prioritized.
- **Last Updated**: Fresh profiles may rank higher to ensure current information.

We do NOT use the term "best" without explaining specific criteria. Payment does NOT affect organic placement. 

## Goreto Editorial Relationship
Top Consultancy Nepal is operated by Goreto Consultancy. To ensure fairness:
- Goreto does not receive artificial ranking boosts in the directory.
- Goreto uses the exact same profile template as any other consultancy.
- Any editorial content (case studies, recommendations) featuring Goreto will be based on genuine reasoning and methodology.

## Sponsorship Disclosure
Consultancies may purchase sponsored placements. 
- All sponsored listings are explicitly labeled as "Sponsored" or "Featured" with clear visual distinctions.
- Sponsored content will never be disguised as organic search results.

## Corrections & Verification
- Users and consultancy owners can report inaccurate information using the "Submit a Correction" form.
- The internal team manually reviews and verifies all correction requests before updating profiles.
- Fake reviews and fabricated claims are strictly prohibited and will be removed upon discovery.
